Blog

Short tips for better shorts.

How to pick moments for a highlight reel

Choose the moments that make sense on their own: a reaction, a surprising fact, a punchline. Keep each one to 3-8 seconds so the video keeps moving, and put the strongest moment first or last.

Captions that keep people watching

Most people watch with the sound off. Short, high-contrast captions with the spoken word highlighted help viewers follow along without reading a wall of text.

Why 40-50 seconds works

It is long enough to tell a small story and short enough that people watch to the end, which platforms tend to reward. A numbered countdown gives viewers a reason to stay for the next one.
